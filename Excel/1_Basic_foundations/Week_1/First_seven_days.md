## **Week 1 — Our first seven days**

Our first week will focus on understanding Excel itself. This may sound basic, but these concepts influence almost everything we do later.

| Day | Topic | Practical outcome |
|-----|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | Excel interface and architecture | Understand the Excel environment |
| 2 | Workbook, worksheets, rows and columns | Navigate and organise workbooks |
| 3 | Cells, ranges and cell references | Understand how Excel identifies data |
| 4 | Data types and data entry | Enter structured business data |
| 5 | Formatting and number formats | Create readable spreadsheets |
| 6 | Excel Tables and basic operations | Structure data professionally |
| 7 | Weekly assessment | Complete a small spreadsheet assignment | 

We'll adjust the following weeks based on our assessment results.
