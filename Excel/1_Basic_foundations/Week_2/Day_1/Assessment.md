- Whole Number only takes Positive integers along with zero as it's value, Whereas the Decimal validations takes positive and negative integers along with fractional values to in decimal forms. e.g.: 0 to 50 (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6....50 as whole number) and -10 to 50 (-10, -4.5, 2, 3.5, 5.5....49.99 as Decimal number) Validation.
