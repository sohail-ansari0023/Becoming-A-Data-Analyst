## **What we will learn in Week 2**

**Day 01:** Data Validation
- Dropdown lists, input restrictions, error alerts, validation rules and preventing incorrect entries.

**Day 02:** Advanced Data Entry
- Flash Fill, Fill Series, AutoFill, Paste Special and efficient data-entry techniques.

**Day 03:** Conditional Formatting
- Highlight Rules, Duplicate Values, Top/Bottom Rules, Data Bars, Color Scales and formula-based formatting.

**Day 04:** Cell References — Part 1
- Relative references and how Excel adjusts formulas when copied.

**Day 05:** Cell References — Part 2
- Absolute refrences ($A$1), F4 shortcut and fixed calculation parameters.

**Day 06:** Cell References — Part 3
- Mixed refrences ($A1 and A$1), practical applications and formula copying.

**Day 07:** Named Ranges
- Creating, managing and using named cells/ranges in business calculations.

**Final Week 2 Assessment**
- Practical Business dataset, formula exercises, error identification and interpretation.
